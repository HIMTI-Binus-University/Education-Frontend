# Public Education Site

Mode: Operate for discovery and schedules; Read for summaries.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A course connects written summaries, scheduled sessions, and recordings. Reproduce the user's approved resource-first reference rather than inventing a marketing site.

OWN-WORLD: HIMTI blue and navy, white panels, pale blue surfaces, Plus Jakarta Sans, restrained round corners. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Semantic course and resource icons only; no decorative background grid.

STORY: Find a course or search a summary title, read its academic context, then navigate to its sessions and recordings. Every fixture is labeled demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating logo/navigation, generous centered two-line headline, supporting text, wide title-search control. Courses below; summary rows next; schedule and recording lists share the lower desktop row and stack on mobile.

FORM: User-pinned screenshot, confirmed in conversation; no random concept selection replaces this reference. Code-led implementation with the screenshot as visual authority.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Decisions
Light mode only to match the approved reference. Focus feedback and subtle hover transitions, no entrance animations. Reuse the existing HIMTI logo, not a newly generated asset. Optional event URLs and recordings are absent unless supplied. Reader rendering supports code and math; authoring remains outside scope.
